1 · Learn

Physics question: helium and argon sit in identical boxes at the same temperature. What is the same about their molecules, what is different, and why does equal temperature not mean equal speed?

Idea 1 — What “rms speed” means

Gas molecules move at many speeds. The useful “typical” speed is the root‑mean‑square speed, vrms. Read the name backwards:

  1. Square each speed.
  2. Mean — average the squares.
  3. Root — take the square root.
vrms = √( (v₁² + v₂² + … + vN²) / N )

Why square first? Kinetic energy is ½mv². Temperature is tied to the average of v², so vrms is the speed that gives the correct average energy: Kavg = ½ m vrms².

Tiny example: speeds 1, 2, 3 m/s. Plain average = 2.00 m/s. Squares 1, 4, 9 → mean 4.67 → root = 2.16 m/s.

Idea 2 — Temperature = average KE

Kavg = (3/2) kB T

No mass in this equation. At 300 K every ideal‑gas molecule has Kavg = 6.21 × 10⁻²¹ J.

Idea 3 — Same KE, different speed

vrms = √(3RT / M)

Mass sits under the root. Argon is 10× heavier than helium, so it moves √10 ≈ 3.16× slower.

Idea 4 — Same pressure

P = N kB T / V

Helium hits the wall 3.16× more often; each argon hit is 3.16× harder. The effects cancel.

Worked example — the assigned case

T = 300 K · MHe = 0.004003 kg/mol · MAr = 0.03995 kg/mol · R = 8.314 J/(mol·K)

3RT = 7483 J/mol → vrms,He = √(7483 ÷ 0.004003) ≈ 1370 m/s; vrms,Ar = √(7483 ÷ 0.03995) ≈ 430 m/s; ratio ≈ 3.16 ✔

4 · Scenarios

Three required investigations. Predict first, then press the button.

A · Typical case (assigned)

Inputs: He vs Ar, 300 K, 0.40 mol, 0.010 m³.

Predict: Kavg = 6.21 × 10⁻²¹ J (both) · vrms ≈ 1370 vs 430 m/s · P ≈ 100 kPa (both).

Evidence & why. The stats read 1370 / 430 m/s, 6.21 × 10⁻²¹ J and 100 kPa — matching the hand values. Same T ⇒ same ½mvrms²; ten times the mass ⇒ √10 less speed; P = NkBT/V has no mass in it.

B · Comparison — sweep one input over five values

B1 — mass. Hold T = 300 K, n, V. Vary mass of Gas B: 4, 20, 40, 80, 160 u. Predict: vrms ∝ 1/√m → ≈ 1370, 610, 430, 310, 220 m/s; Kavg flat at 6.21 × 10⁻²¹ J.

B2 — temperature. Hold mass = 39.95 u, n, V. Vary T: 100, 200, 300, 400, 500 K. Predict: vrms ∝ √T; vrms² vs T is a straight line through the origin for each gas.

Evidence & why. B1: the points fall on a 1/√m curve while Kavg stays constant — temperature alone sets energy. B2: vrms² vs T gives two lines with slopes 3kB/m that never cross — no temperature makes the speeds equal.

C · Boundary cases

C1 · 50 K. Predict He ≈ 560, Ar ≈ 180 m/s. The maths works, but argon freezes at 84 K — outside the model's scope (the stats panel flags this).

C2 · 1000 K. Predict He ≈ 2500, Ar ≈ 790 m/s, P ≈ 333 kPa. Valid — both stay ideal gases.

Why. The equations never break; the assumptions do. No phase changes or quantum effects are modelled.

5 · Model

Concept, what the parts mean, rules, assumptions, limitations.

Concept

Kinetic theory of an ideal gas: temperature measures average translational KE per molecule; speed then follows from mass.

Equations → where they act

EquationUsed for
Kavg = (3/2)kBTEnergy stat (same for both)
vrms = √(3kBT/m)Speed stat, dashed lines, dot motion
Maxwell–Boltzmann f(v)Distribution curves, dot speeds
PV = NkBTPressure gauges

Rules

  1. One T, n, V for both boxes; He fixed at 4.003 u, Gas B mass adjustable (argon = 39.95 u).
  2. All outputs recomputed on every slider move — nothing is pre‑animated.
  3. Dot speeds sampled from f(v); same pixel scale for both boxes.

What you see

ElementMeaning
Grey boxesEqual‑volume containers (the systems)
Orange barShared reservoir fixing T
40 dotsMolecules; equal count = equal N
Green barsPressure

Assumptions — and why

  • Ideal, monatomic gas: He and Ar near 1 atm obey PV = nRT to ~1 %.
  • Equilibrium: both have reached reservoir temperature.
  • Equal N and V: as assigned, isolating mass.

Limitations

  • No phase changes (Ar liquid below 87 K) — floor 50 K.
  • No quantum effects or intermolecular collisions.
  • Dots are schematic and 2‑D.

Misconception

“Same temperature ⇒ same speed.” Temperature fixes ½mv², not v. Helium races, argon crawls, yet Kavg and P match exactly.
